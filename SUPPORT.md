# Support

This project is maintained as an open-source pre-validation prototype. No response-time
or service-level commitment is provided.

## Choose a channel

Select the channel that matches the type and sensitivity of the request.

- **Bug or unexpected behavior:** open a
  [bug report](https://github.com/owans/docs-agent-readiness-toolkit/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml).
- **Documentation problem:** open a
  [documentation issue](https://github.com/owans/docs-agent-readiness-toolkit/issues/new?template=documentation.yml).
- **Feature or scope proposal:** open a
  [feature request](https://github.com/owans/docs-agent-readiness-toolkit/issues/new?template=feature_request.yml).
- **Security vulnerability:** follow [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md). Do not use a public
  issue for sensitive details.
- **Contribution question:** review [`CONTRIBUTING.md`](CONTRIBUTING.md) and open an
  issue if the guidance does not answer it.

## Before filing

Include the toolkit revision, Node.js version, command, sanitized configuration,
expected behavior, observed behavior, and a minimal generic fixture when possible.
Remove credentials, private documentation, internal URLs, deployment identifiers, and
participant information.

Requests for deferred capabilities may be discussed, but they do not change the
current product boundary. Practitioner validation remains the next gate before live
acquisition or broad expansion.
